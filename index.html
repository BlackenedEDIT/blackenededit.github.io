How We Build

No energy timers. No forced ads.

Every Pixel Inc title is designed around fair progression and immediate gameplay.

01

Optional Rewarded Ads Only

We never interrupt a run or mining trip with forced interstitials. Rewarded ads are strictly opt-in when you want a revive or bonus.

02

Skill Over Spend

High scores, vault clears, and arcade trophies are earned through timing, knowledge, and practice — never pay-to-win shortcuts.

03

Local-First & Offline Ready

No mandatory accounts or always-online DRM. Pick up your phone anywhere and jump straight back into your save.
