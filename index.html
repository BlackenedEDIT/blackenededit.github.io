What is Gemhollow?

Gemhollow is a hand-painted mining RPG where every block is yours to break. You start with a basic pickaxe and fifteen pockets of carrying space, and you dig — sideways, downward, wherever the ore is — placing your own ladders, ceiling supports and bridging beams to reach seams nobody else can.

Haul what you find back to the surface, sell it on a market whose prices actually move, and turn the money into a better pickaxe, a bigger pack, a brighter lamp. Then go deeper, because the good stuff is always deeper.

Two full worlds, 56 story quests, six hand-built puzzle vaults, pets, robot miners and a contracts board — and the whole thing is free, with rewarded ads that are always optional.

The Worlds

Two complete mines, each 500 metres deep, each with its own ores, tools, hazards and economy.

World I

The Surface Mine

Home base is a lantern-lit village — Mine Control, the Market, the Item Shop, the Gear Shop — built around the portal that drops you into the shaft. Stone gives way to iron, then gold, then diamond, and the rare stuff starts appearing once you're past a hundred metres down.

  • Quartz from 25m, Ruby from 80m, Sapphire from 160m
  • Prismatic Cores from 200m, Aetherium from 250m
  • The Elder Gate waits at 240m
  • 40 chests per mine, golden ones below 150m
World II

The Abyss

Through the gate, everything is harder and worth more. Abyssal Rock takes ten times the swings of surface stone, toxic gas pockets bleed your vitality until you've earned a rebreather, and the ores are corrupted mirrors of the ones you knew — Shadow Iron, Void Diamond, Star Sapphire.

  • Toxic gas: 15 vitality/second without a mask
  • Filter Mask cuts it to 5/s, Rebreather to zero
  • Void-Sealed Rock and Obsidian Barriers gate the descent
  • Dark Aetherium sells for $5,000 a unit
Core Gameplay

Game Systems

Not a feature list — these are the actual systems running in the game right now.

A Mine You Reshape

500 rows deep, 50 columns wide, procedurally generated and completely destructible. Tunnel any direction and place ladders, ceiling supports and bridging beams to build your own route down — then fall down your own shaft if you're careless.

12 Skill Minigames

Hit ore and you earn the yield. Time the Iron Striker, purify gold, align diamond fissures, hold a harmonic resonance — nail it perfectly for five units instead of one, plus a bonus gem roll.

Six Puzzle Vaults

Hand-built labyrinths, not random rooms. Dodge flame jets, ride wind geysers, survive a gas maze, pick the vault lock — each one guards a Keystone the campaign needs.

Company Contracts

Daily, weekly and monthly jobs from the Gemhollow Mining Company that scale with your pickaxe tier. Clear 40 in a month and you keep that month's exclusive aura for good.

Ten Pets

Buy them with gems, not cash. A Quartz Dog adds speed and ladders, a Ruby Cat doubles every ore you collect, a Sapphire Bat throws away spare stone so your pack stays useful.

Robot Miners

Donate ore to build a fleet of twelve automated units, from a Stone Excavator up to an Aetheric Unit at six times the yield. They keep digging while the app is closed — up to twelve hours on a full power cell.

A Market That Moves

Ore prices drift, surge and recover in real time — dump too much of one ore and you depress its price, or wait for a demand event and double your money. Add a 30-day login ladder, stacking boosts, 17 auras and 38 Play Games achievements on top.

Hand-Built Challenges

The Vaults

Six of them, each a designed puzzle with its own hazard and its own Keystone at the end.

I

The Vault of Earth

A pitch-dark procedural labyrinth. You get 99 ladders and 25 beams — the challenge is navigating the maze and solving the gate sequence, not rationing supplies.

Keystone of Earth
II

The Vault of Fire

Fifty-two rows of hand-tuned maze threaded with flame-jet emitters and magma rock that detonates when struck. Reach the venting console at the top to shut it down.

Keystone of Fire
III

The Vault of Sky

Seventy-five rows straight up. Build platforms over wind geysers, ride the updraft, land the jump — miss and you start the climb again.

Keystone of Sky
IV

The Toxic Vault

Twenty ladders, five gas pockets, and a golden chest at the far end. Break the wrong block and the fog comes for you.

Golden Chest
V

The Abyssal Vault

A tight bridging puzzle in the deep dark, with a Keystone of Fire waiting at the end of a long horizontal run.

Abyss · World II
VI

The Void Vault

Five wind geysers, fifty beams, fifty ladders and a Keystone of Sky. The last vault before the Overworld Interface.

Abyss · World II
Look The Part

Twenty-One Miners

Cosmetic appearances, not classes — every miner plays identically, so nothing is locked behind a look. Swap them at the Character Market.

Male & Female Miner

Quest

Prismatic Miner

Day 30

Golden Miner

The prize at the end of the 30-day login ladder. Miss a day and the streak skips — no shortcuts.

Secret

A rare roll from supply crates. Nobody sells it and no quest grants it — you get lucky, or you don't.

Part of the Founders Pack, at the game's largest visual scale. There's a corrupted Abyss variant too.
